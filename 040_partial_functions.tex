\section{Partial Function Semantics Following PFOL}

\paragraph{Status.}
This is a secondary draft for preserved Alternative 4, not the active total
comprehension evaluation. The authoritative historical source is
\texttt{210\_partial\_functions\_semantics.tex}.

For a univocal relation $f$, the PFOL notation is
\[
f(x)\simeq(\iota y,\langle x,y\rangle\in f).
\]
The descriptor is defined exactly when its witness exists uniquely:
\[
(\iota y,\varphi)\downarrow\iff(\exists!y,\varphi).
\]
Kleene equality $\simeq$ and the definedness predicate are part of this
alternative partial logic. They must not be identified silently with the
active classical equality and total union-based family evaluation.
