\begin{corollary}[alternative induction principle on $R_1$--well--founded class]
This formulation of inductive principle corresponds to the exercise 9.22 of ``Introduction to Axiomatic Set Theory''(Takeuti,Zaring).
\[
(R_1\mathrm{\ Fr\ } K)
\implies
(
((K\cap(\power_{(R_1\mathrm{\ on\ }K)} A))\subclass A)
\implies
(K\subclass A)
)
\labeltagd{thm}{RindAlt}
\]
\Proof
Assume $(R_1\mathrm{\ on\ }K)$.\\
Let be $R\ceq (R_1\mathrm{\ on\ }K)$.\\
By lemma \myref{lem:restrreltran}, $((R_1\mathrm{\ on\ }K)\mathrm{\ Tr\ }K)$.\\
I.e. $(R\mathrm{\ Tr\ }K)$.\\
Therefore, $(R\mathrm{\ Fr\ }K)
\implies
(
((K\cap(\power_R A))\subclass A)
\implies
(K\subclass A)
)
$ by theorem \myref{thm:Rind}.\\

Since $R\subclass R_1$(it follows from the local definition of $R$), then by theorem \myref{thm:inheritable}
\[
(R_1\mathrm{\ on\ }K)
\implies
(R\mathrm{\ on\ }K)
\]
This allows us to obtain the following:
\[
(
((K\cap(\power_R A))\subclass A)
\implies
(K\subclass A)
)
.\]
Finally, we have $(
((K\cap(\power_{(R_1\mathrm{\ on\ }K)} A))\subclass A)
\implies
(K\subclass A)
)$ by unfolding $R_1$.
\Done
\end{corollary}
