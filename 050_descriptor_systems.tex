\section{Descriptor Systems}

\paragraph{Status.}
These are secondary notes for the preserved descriptor and PFOL alternatives,
not a unified active descriptor system.

\subsection{Class Abstraction}
In class theory, $\cc{x}{\varphi}$ denotes a class expression. It is not
automatically a set: sethood requires a separate proof. In particular, no
set or class of all classes may be introduced by this notation.

\subsection{PFOL Iota Notation}
Within Farmer's PFOL alternative, the iota descriptor satisfies
\[
(\iota x,\varphi)\downarrow\iff(\exists!x,\varphi).
\]
When defined, it denotes the unique witness. This statement belongs to the
partial logic of \texttt{210\_partial\_functions\_semantics.tex}; it is not an
axiom of the active total union-based evaluation.

The three-valued choice conventions remain in
\texttt{020\_descriptors.tex} and \texttt{180\_many-valued.tex}. Their
$\tau$, $\epsilon$, and partial $\varepsilon$ operators must not be collapsed
into one operator.
