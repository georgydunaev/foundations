\subsection{Regular-Open Completion and Boolean-Valued Models}
\label{subsec:ro_completion_boolean_valued_models}

Given a forcing preorder $(\mathbb{P}, \le, 1_{\mathbb{P}})$, for any subset $U \subseteq \mathbb{P}$ let $U^\perp \eqdef \cc{q \in \mathbb{P}}{\forall p \in U,\ \neg(p \parallel q)}$ denote the incompatible complement, and let $U^{\perp\perp} \eqdef (U^\perp)^\perp$ denote the regular-open closure. The regular-open completion $\mathrm{ro}(\mathbb{P}) \eqdef \cc{U \subseteq \mathbb{P}}{U = U^{\perp\perp}}$ ordered by inclusion forms a complete Boolean algebra $(\mathbb{B}, \wedge, \vee, \neg, 0_{\mathbb{B}}, 1_{\mathbb{B}})$ with $U \wedge W = U \cap W$, $U \vee W = (U \cup W)^{\perp\perp}$, and $\neg U = U^\perp$. The canonical map $e(p) \eqdef (\downarrow p)^{\perp\perp}$ embeds $\mathbb{P}$ densely into $\mathbb{B} \setminus \{0_{\mathbb{B}}\}$ while preserving order and incompatibility ($p \perp q \iff e(p) \wedge e(q) = 0_{\mathbb{B}}$).

A $\mathbb{B}$-name $u \in V^{(\mathbb{B})}$ is a set of ordered pairs $\pair{\sigma}{b}$ with $\sigma \in V^{(\mathbb{B})}$ and $b \in \mathbb{B}$. The Boolean truth values $\llbracket u \in v \rrbracket_{\mathbb{B}}$ and $\llbracket u = v \rrbracket_{\mathbb{B}}$ are defined by mutual well-founded recursion:
\[
\llbracket u \in v \rrbracket_{\mathbb{B}} \eqdef \bigvee_{\pair{\sigma}{b} \in v} \big(b \wedge \llbracket u = \sigma \rrbracket_{\mathbb{B}}\big), \qquad
\llbracket u = v \rrbracket_{\mathbb{B}} \eqdef \bigwedge_{\pair{\sigma}{b} \in u} \big(b \Rightarrow \llbracket \sigma \in v \rrbracket_{\mathbb{B}}\big) \wedge \bigwedge_{\pair{\tau}{c} \in v} \big(c \Rightarrow \llbracket \tau \in u \rrbracket_{\mathbb{B}}\big),
\]
under which all ZFC axioms evaluate to $1_{\mathbb{B}}$ and the forcing relation $p \Vdash \phi$ over $\mathbb{P}$-names $V^{\mathbb{P}}$ satisfies $p \Vdash \phi \iff e(p) \le \llbracket \phi \rrbracket_{\mathrm{ro}(\mathbb{P})}$.

\subsection{Normal Ordinal Class Functions, Veblen Hierarchy, Club Filter, and Fodor's Lemma}
\label{subsec:normal_functions_veblen_club_fodor}

An ordinal class function $F\colon \mathrm{Ord} \to \mathrm{Ord}$ whose graph is encoded by ordered pairs $\pair{\alpha}{F(\alpha)}$ is \emph{normal} if it is strictly increasing ($\alpha < \beta \implies F(\alpha) < F(\beta)$) and continuous at every limit ordinal $\lambda > 0$ ($F(\lambda) = \sup_{\alpha < \lambda} F(\alpha)$). Every normal class function enumerates a proper class of fixed points $\gamma = F(\gamma)$, iterating into the Veblen hierarchy $\varphi_\alpha(\beta)$ where $\varphi_0(\beta) = \omega^\beta$ and, for $\alpha > 0$, $\varphi_\alpha$ enumerates the common fixed points of $\varphi_\gamma$ for all $\gamma < \alpha$.

For any regular uncountable cardinal $\kappa$, a subset $C \subseteq \kappa$ is \emph{closed unbounded} ($\mathrm{Club}(C, \kappa)$) if $C$ is unbounded in $\kappa$ and contains every limit ordinal $\lambda < \kappa$ for which $\sup(C \cap \lambda) = \lambda$; a subset $S \subseteq \kappa$ is \emph{stationary} if $S \cap C \neq \emptyclass$ for every club $C \subseteq \kappa$. Given a $\kappa$-sequence of clubs $(C_\alpha)_{\alpha < \kappa}$ represented via pairs $\pair{\alpha}{C_\alpha}$, the diagonal intersection $\Delta_{\alpha < \kappa} C_\alpha \eqdef \cc{\beta < \kappa}{\forall \alpha < \beta,\ \beta \in C_\alpha}$ is again a club in $\kappa$. Consequently, Fodor's Pressing-Down Lemma holds: every regressive function $f\colon S \setminus \{\emptyclass\} \to \kappa$ (satisfying $f(\alpha) < \alpha$ for all $\alpha \in S \setminus \{\emptyclass\}$) on a stationary $S \subseteq \kappa$ is constant on a stationary subset $S_0 \subseteq S$.

\subsection{Gale-Stewart Games and Borel Determinacy}
\label{subsec:gale_stewart_borel_determinacy}

A pruned game tree $T$ of finite positions is represented by finite sequences closed under prefixes and without terminal dead-ends. For a payoff class $A \subseteq [T]$ of infinite plays, the two-player Gale-Stewart game $G(T, A)$ alternates moves between Player~I (even lengths) and Player~II (odd lengths). Strategies $\sigma_{\mathrm{I}}$ and $\sigma_{\mathrm{II}}$ are encoded as graphs of pairs $\pair{p}{m}$ mapping legal positions $p \in T$ to next moves $m$. By strategy consistency, Player~I and Player~II cannot simultaneously possess winning strategies in $G(T, A)$. Gale-Stewart Determinacy establishes that $G(T, A)$ is determined whenever $A$ is open or closed in the product topology on $[T]$, and Martin's Borel Determinacy Theorem extends determinacy via transfinite induction on Borel rank (using replacement and powerset closure in $\V$) to every Borel payoff $A \in \mathbf{\Delta}^1_1([T])$.
