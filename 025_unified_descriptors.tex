\section{[OUTDATED] Unified Descriptor Draft}

\paragraph{Status.}
This file is retained only as a non-normative historical draft. It does not
define a descriptor semantics and is not part of the active kernel. The former
idea of packing domains, choice operators, PFOL, and a type-semantics label
into one tuple was withdrawn: such a tuple specifies neither truth,
definedness, equality, nor translation laws.

The motivating question remains useful: what information must be supplied
before two descriptor systems can be compared? At minimum, a comparison
needs the domain of values, the treatment of missing and multiple witnesses,
and the interpretation of equality and substitution. The sources below
answer different parts of that question in their own settings.

\subsection{Preserved Descriptor Sources}
The actual alternatives remain separate and intact:
\begin{itemize}
\item \texttt{020\_descriptors.tex} and \texttt{180\_many-valued.tex}
  distinguish $\tau\ceq\tau^{\Un}$,
  $\epsilon\ceq\tau^{\{\emptyclass\}}$, and the partial
  $\varepsilon\ceq\tau^{\emptyclass}$;
\item \texttt{210\_partial\_functions\_semantics.tex} uses PFOL
  iota-definedness and Kleene equality;
\item \texttt{030\_alternative\_semantics.tex} preserves the independent
  conglomerate-valued assignment semantics.
\end{itemize}

The active kernel instead uses total class-comprehension evaluation as
documented in \texttt{presentation.ML} and \texttt{060\_axioms.tex}. No
compatibility or conservativity claim between these systems is made here.
