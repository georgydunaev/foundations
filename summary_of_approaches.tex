This document summarizes the key concepts and differences from the various approaches to foundations presented in the book.

\subsection{Primary Semantics}
The primary semantics approach defines the basic concepts of the theory, including terms, types, and families. It provides a foundational framework for the other approaches. Key concepts include:\n- Terms and their properties\n- Types as unary predicates\n- Families as dependent types\n- The concept of a class as a collection of terms
